## Scope

Whole site: home, project and post pages, listings, About. Mode: **Read** (home is a document index, not a pitch). Audience: geospatial and DT practitioners on phones over mobile data. Job: find a case study or article, read it comfortably, follow to related work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every page is a sheet in a topographic map series. The site refuses the hero-plus-card-grid portfolio and the plain blog column.

OWN-WORLD: Map-paper ground (cool off-white; night-map dark), deep forest-teal ink, terracotta index contours, muted grey collar text. Neatline frame, graticule ticks, condensed-caps Archivo collar labels, Source Serif 4 reading column, JetBrains Mono for figures. A contour field generated from each slug: teal hairlines plus a terracotta index line every fifth.

STORY: The reader sees two practices (Geospatial AI, Digital Transformation) as one series of sheets, locates projects on the Indonesia index, and reads honest methods.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: title block top-left (positioning line), sheet-index map of Indonesia filling the right/below with numbered regions; legend lists the two entry points; series list beside it. Post: collar (sheet no., category legend, reading-time scale bar), contour masthead, then title.

FORM: Brief-pinned (PRD "field notebook meets topographic map"); no seed roll. Signature interaction: list-to-map linked highlight.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
